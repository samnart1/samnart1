What a circuit breaker actually costs you

MSc thesis · 20 conditions · 150 replication runs · about 90 hours of measurement

In the world of Software Engineering, most engineers agree that circuit breakers need to be used in large systems like Microservices. Yet, almost nobody has taken the time to measure what happens when circuit breakers are used. This thesis investigates and measures a ten service shop, at five failure intensities (0, 25, 50, 75, 100). The experiment was ran twice on two laptops and then 15 times on one laptop. The simple and short version is that, circuits breakers do not remove cost, but they move it. And they move the cost to things that we probably never expected.

Below is an interactive working model of the system experimented on.
