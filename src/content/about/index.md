I'm Sam, a backend and distributed systems engineer finishing an MSc in
Software Engineering at the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano. I build the
unglamorous parts that have to not fall over: storage engines, data
pipelines, and services that stay up when things go wrong.

Most of my work lives close to the metal, C++ and Go for the systems that
need to be fast and correct, Python and TypeScript for everything around
them. I care about code that survives contact with production.

Originally from Ghana, now in northern Italy. Open to backend roles with
visa sponsorship.
