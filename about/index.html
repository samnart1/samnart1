About

I am a software engineer focused on backend and distributed systems. I am finishing an MSc in Software Engineering and I like building things that have to be correct and fast at the same time.

Most of my work lives on the backend: data pipelines, APIs, and the infrastructure that runs them. My stack is Go and Python with FastAPI and Postgres, deployed on Azure with Kubernetes, Docker and CI/CD. I do enough React and TypeScript to build the control panels my systems need.

My thesis measured the performance and energy cost of resilience patterns in microservices, which is a good summary of what I care about: not just whether a system works, but what it costs to make it work well.

Outside of engineering I follow football and rockets, and I am slowly learning German. I am based in Bolzano, in the north of Italy.
